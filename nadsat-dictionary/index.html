About this Govoreet

Nadsat is a fictional slang dialect used by the teenagers in Anthony Burgess's 1962 novel A Clockwork Orange.

Rather than a complete language, it is an English-based argot where many nouns, verbs, and adjectives are replaced with words derived from Russian (e.g. droog from друг for friend, baboochka from бабушка for grandmother), Cockney rhyming slang, and schoolboy jargon.
